Masjidمسجد

→

Birzeit, Palestine

A village mosque at the edge of Birzeit, organized around a single threshold: a shaded colonnade that reads as public space before it reads as sacred, beneath a folded concrete roof that lifts toward the qibla wall to draw one band of clerestory light down the hall.

Bandبند

→

Al-Bireh, Palestine

On a narrow infill lot in Al-Bireh, a folded concrete band wraps stair, corridor and balcony into one continuous move, freeing every room around it and stepping outward at each floor to shade the storey below.

Residential

Omaryعمري

→

Al-Baloo', Palestine

Set on a steeply sloped plot in Al-Baloo', Omary steps down the grade in three shallow terraces rather than fighting it with retaining walls, giving each level its own outdoor room and letting three generations share one address without sharing every room.

Residential

Sakhrصخر

→

Al-Bireh, Palestine

Sakhr — ‘rock’ in Arabic — turns Al-Bireh's common load-bearing stone construction into the building's main idea: a perforated stone screen wraps the south and west faces, filtering light before it reaches the glazing behind while the apartment plan stays simple.

Residential

ِAbdallah Al-Jamalعبدالله الجمل

→

Ramallah, Palestine

A family compound in Ramallah split into three linked volumes — one for the parents, two for married children — arranged around a shared entrance court so each household keeps its own front door, with a single roof terrace tying them together above.

Residential

Abdallah Ewias عبدالله عويس

→

Al-Bireh, Palestine

On a corner lot in Al-Bireh, Abdallah Ewias gives both streets a genuine front — two entrances and two living rooms folded around a shared internal stair — a move that also opens an unplanned triangular garden at the back of the site.

Residential

Bireh Plazaساحة البلد

→

City Center, Al-Bireh

Bireh Plaza cuts a public plaza through the middle of a dense city-center block, turning one deep building footprint into a shortcut pedestrians actually want to take, with shops at eye level and offices and a small event hall stacked above.

Cultural - Commercial

Iyyad Jaradatاياد جردات

→

Ramallah, Palestine

The Iyyad Jaradat building in Ramallah stacks three floors of apartments over a glazed commercial ground floor, keeping the structural transfer level between them visible rather than hidden, with deep residential balconies that double as sun-shading for the units below.
