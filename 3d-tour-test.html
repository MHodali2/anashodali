01 — Arrival

A deep city-center block, opened up

Bireh Plaza reworks a dense block in the middle of Al-Bireh into ground-floor retail with a public plaza cut straight through it — turning a single deep footprint into a shortcut people actually want to take.

The walkthrough comes in from the south, where the tower and the podium first read as one mass.

02 — The cut

“A plaza only works if it's also the fastest way to get somewhere.”

Shops line the new plaza at eye level; offices and a small event hall sit above. The plaza itself is treated as the project's real civic contribution — deliberately more generous than the retail alone requires.

03 — The west entrance

Crossing the threshold

The route picks the revolving door on the western face as its way in — the one unambiguous civic threshold on the model — and drops to eye level to pass through it.

As the camera crosses, the roof and tower section away overhead so the ground floor stays readable.

04 — Under the canopy

The covered arcade

Inside, the path follows the colonnade that runs off the entrance: a rhythm of columns and soffit beams, the shopfronts to one side, the plaza opening out to the other.

A steel canopy shades the plaza without enclosing it; the flanking buildings are finished in a warm limestone that ties the project to the older city-center facades around it.

05 — Looking back

Retail below, offices above, a route through the middle

The camera lifts back out at the far end and turns to take in the whole block — the piece the plaza was carved out of.
