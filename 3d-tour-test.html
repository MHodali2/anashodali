01 — The block

A tower, a stepped podium, and a plaza at the centre

Bireh Plaza reworks a dense block in the middle of Al-Bireh: an office tower rising from a terraced podium of retail, with an open plaza cut through at ground level.

The walkthrough begins high to the south-east, where the whole composition reads at once.

02 — Arrival

Coming down to the street

The camera drops toward the southern edge of the site and lines up with the plaza — the point where the public space opens off the surrounding streets.

03 — The plaza

“A plaza only works if it's also the fastest way to get somewhere.”

Shops line the plaza at eye level, stepping back in terraces toward the tower. The plaza is treated as the project's real civic contribution — deliberately more generous than the retail alone requires.

04 — The tower

Retail below, offices above

From the plaza the view lifts and pulls back along the tower's east face: projecting slab edges mark the floors, and a glazed skin wraps the offices above the podium.

05 — Around the block

Over the roof, and back down

The route crosses above the roof and circles the north and west sides, seeing the tower as the free-standing object it is before coming back to the plaza it sits above.
